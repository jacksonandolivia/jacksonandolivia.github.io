Explore Ames

Outdoors and Around Town

  • Downtown Ames

    Spend some time strolling Main Street where you'll find plenty of local shops, restaurants, bars, and coffee.

  • Ada Hayden Heritage Park

    A favorite spot for a walk around the lake, with prairie views.

  • Brookside Park

    Spacious park near campus with walking paths, mature trees, and a play area for kids.

  • Inis Grove Park

    Neighborhood park with walking paths and an extensive play area.

  • Iowa State University

    Explore Iowa State's campus, right across the street from St. Thomas Aquinas Church.

  • Reiman Gardens

    Wander the gardens and visit the indoor butterfly wing. There is a small admission fee for entry.

Breweries in Ames

Breweries & Drinks

  • Alluvial Brewing Company

    Our favorite local brewery surrounded by prairie, with a great outdoor space and a rotating selection of craft beer.

  • London Underground

    A cozy, quirky pub on Main Street with cocktails and a selection of European beers.

  • Torrent Brewing Company

    Casual downtown brewery with food (we recommend the pretzel!).

Breakfast in Ames

Breakfast & Coffee

  • Lockwood Cafe

    Sweet and savory crêpes and good coffee in an old grain elevator.

  • Morning Bell Coffee

    Our local coffee favorite.

  • Tosca

    Campustown café with delicious breakfast and coffee.

  • The Cafe

    A longtime local favorite with seasonal dishes and fresh-baked pastries and bread.

Dinner in Ames

Dinner

  • Tosca

    Italian-inspired dishes and a fun bar.

  • The Great Plains Sauce & Dough Co.

    An Ames classic known for its thick-crust pizza and old-school charm.

  • The Mucky Duck Pub

    Cozy British-style pub with classic fish and chips and a wide selection of beer and cider.

  • Provisions Lot F

    Great spot for a nicer dinner, with a seasonal menu and house-made bread.

  • Cornbred Barbecue

    Barbecue and classic comfort food.

  • The Cafe

    Seasonal dishes, cocktails, and a well-stocked bakery.

  • Hickory Park

    An Ames institution for barbecue and burgers, with an extensive ice cream menu.
