Explore Ames

Outdoors and Around Town

  • Ada Hayden Heritage Park

    A favorite spot for a walk around the lake, with prairie views and plenty of room to enjoy the outdoors.

  • Brookside Park

    A beautiful, shady park near campus with walking paths, mature trees, and lots of green space.

  • Inis Grove Park

    A spacious neighborhood park with wooded areas, walking trails, and plenty of places to slow down and enjoy a little time outside.

  • Iowa State University

    Take a walk around Iowa State’s beautiful campus, especially Central Campus and the area around the Campanile.

  • Downtown Ames

    Spend some time strolling Main Street, where you’ll find local shops, restaurants, bars, coffee, and plenty of small-town charm.

  • Reiman Gardens

    Spend some time wandering the gardens and be sure to visit the indoor butterfly wing.

Breweries in Ames

Breweries & Drinks

  • Alluvial Brewing Company

    Our favorite local brewery surrounded by prairie, with a great outdoor space and a rotating selection of craft beer.

  • London Underground

    A cozy, quirky pub on Main Street with cocktails, beer, whiskey, and the kind of atmosphere that makes you want to stay awhile.

  • Torrent Brewing Company

    A casual downtown brewery with plenty of space to settle in and try a few locally brewed beers.

Breakfast in Ames

Breakfast & Brunch

  • Lockwood Cafe

    A relaxed and eclectic café known for coffee, sweet and savory crêpes, and a wonderfully tucked-away atmosphere.

  • Morning Bell Coffee

    A local coffee favorite with a cozy atmosphere, great espresso drinks, and a perfect stop for a relaxed morning in Ames.

  • Tosca

    A warm, European-inspired café in Campustown that's a lovely spot for breakfast, coffee, or a leisurely brunch.

  • The Cafe

    A longtime favorite for breakfast and brunch, with seasonal dishes, fresh-baked pastries and bread, and plenty of good coffee.

Dinner in Ames

Dinner

  • Tosca

    A cozy choice for dinner in Campustown, with Italian-inspired dishes, cocktails, and a relaxed café atmosphere.

  • The Cafe

    A great dinner option for seasonal dishes, cocktails, and something sweet from the bakery before you leave.

  • Provisions Lot F

    A polished but relaxed spot for a nicer dinner, with seasonal dishes, house-made bread and pastries, and a good cocktail list.

  • The Great Plains Sauce & Dough Co.

    An Ames classic for hearty pizza—try one of the signature thick crusts and save the crust for a drizzle of honey.

  • Hickory Park

    An Ames institution for barbecue and classic comfort food, with a famously extensive dessert menu that's worth saving room for.
